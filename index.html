01The house

A workshop that grew
without changing hands.

Incense Creators was founded in 1989 by Shri B. S. Narayan Murthy, with the blessings of his father, Shri Anandramaiah Shetty. The first year ran on four employees and one conviction: that an incense stick is only as good as the perfume it carries.

Thirty-six years on, the brand sells across India — strongest through the South — and ships to buyers in more than ten countries. The bench is bigger. The test has not changed: light one, and listen to the room.

  • Recognition“Divine Incense Creator”, awarded to Shri B. S. Narayan Murthy for best businessman in small-scale industry.
  • New DelhiNiryat Shiromani Puraskar, from the Indian Council of Small and Medium Exporters.
  • 2003Gold Medal for exports.

03Making

Five stages, in order.

None of this is unusual for good agarbatti. Doing all five properly on every batch is the part that isn't common.

  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Bamboo slivers, charcoal, jigat binder and natural aromatics bought to a fixed spec and checked on arrival.

  2. 02

    Blending

    The perfume is composed and matured before it meets a stick. This is where a line gets its character.

  3. 03

    Rolling

    Masala is rolled onto the sliver to a consistent weight, so burn time holds steady across the pack.

  4. 04

    Curing

    Sticks are dried and rested. Rushing this stage is the commonest reason incense smells flat.

  5. 05

    Burn test

    Batches are lit and assessed, then packed for retail or export — including under your own label.
